# Flieva skits — week 1 (Google Flow / Veo)

House rules
- Characters are fictional and play scenes. No one poses as a real customer or gives a "testimonial".
- Flieva is shown by the brand host (Kemi) or as the obvious punchline, never as a fake review.
- Captions never say AI. Switch on the platform's own "AI-generated" setting for these videos (TikTok, IG, YouTube require it for realistic people).
- Every skit ends with the Flieva end card (blue, logo, "flieva.com").
- Vertical 9:16, Lagos settings, Nigerian accents, light Pidgin.

## Cast (create once in Flow as characters)
- **@OgaSmart** — man, about 50, heavy build, shiny purple agbada, big gold watch, sunglasses on forehead. Deep raspy fast-talking voice, salesman energy.
- **@Tunde** — man, about 25, slim, round glasses, grey hoodie. Calm, dry, sceptical voice.
- **@MamaT** — woman, about 55, bright orange gele and wrapper. Loud, dramatic, sing-song voice.
- **@Ada** — woman, about 22, long braids, yellow top. Quick, sarcastic, bright voice.
- **@Kemi** — woman, about 28, street interviewer, short natural hair, blue jacket, microphone with a blue foam cover. Warm, punchy presenter voice.

## Skit 1 — "Oga Smart's $10,000 visa" (4 clips × 8 s)
1. Busy Lagos roadside, plastic table under a beach umbrella, handwritten sign "VISA ABROAD 100% GUARANTEE". @OgaSmart leans in to @Tunde: "My brother, ten thousand dollars. Visa, job, ticket, even jacket for London cold!"
2. @Tunde, unimpressed: "Which visa route? Which form? Where is the official fee?" @OgaSmart freezes, then: "Form? ...Form na for school."
3. @Tunde holds up his phone: "The government publishes the fee. I pay the government. Not your personal account." @OgaSmart's smile drops.
4. @OgaSmart folds the table in one move, grabs the umbrella and runs off down the street. @Tunde turns to camera, deadpan: "Check before you pay."

## Skit 2 — "Mama wants to see the plan" (4 clips)
1. Living room, evening. @MamaT stands, hands on hips: "Tunde! I hear say you wan japa. Show me your plan."
2. @Tunde scrolls his phone proudly: "Mummy, I have forty-seven screenshots."
3. @MamaT, horrified, slaps her forehead: "This is not a plan. This is a GALLERY!"
4. @Ada walks in holding her phone: "Mummy, my checklist is here. Passport done. Bank statement next." @MamaT turns to Tunde: "Why can't you be like your sister?"

## Skit 3 — "How much is a UK visa?" street interview (4 clips)
1. Busy Lagos market, @Kemi to a young man in a football jersey: "How much is a UK visa?" Him, confidently: "Five million naira!"
2. @Kemi to a woman with shopping bags: same question. Her: "E depend on your village people."
3. @Kemi to an older man on a bench. Him, chuckling: "Free, if you know somebody."
4. @Kemi to camera, holding up her phone: "Nobody knew. The real fee is on the official government website. Flieva gives you the link and the fee for your route. Stop guessing."

## Skit 4 — "The WhatsApp aunty" (3 clips)
1. @MamaT reading her phone aloud: "Canada is giving free visa to everybody, click this link before midnight!"
2. @Ada leans over: "Mummy, paste it in the scam check first." Taps phone. Phone screen glows red.
3. @MamaT, offended, to camera: "So my own WhatsApp group is lying to me?"
